S26 Ultra power telemetry assessment - 2026-11-14

Result
Battery-interface voltage/current and Samsung system-current telemetry work.
Individual CPU/GPU/HTP rail energy was not exposed through the tested interfaces.
Whole-device wattage has not been calibrated against an external measurement.

Measured phase averages (32 samples each)
                     Idle          CPU load       Recovery
Battery voltage       3.951 V       3.952 V        3.954 V
Battery current      +465.3 mA     -754.2 mA      +468.7 mA
Battery temperature   26.9 C        26.9 C         27.0 C
System current ISYS    95.2 mA     1338.9 mA        96.9 mA
Signed battery V*I    +1.838 W      -2.980 W       +1.853 W

The last row is a calculated battery-interface product, NOT total device power.
The cell is removed, Monsoon supplies the battery strip, and PC USB is connected.
Consequently battery current does not account for every supply path. Positive
current is the gauge's charging-direction convention, not evidence of an actual
cell charging. State of charge and remaining-capacity estimates are not useful
physical battery capacity in this setup. Preserve current sign when analyzing it.

The CPU phase ran the existing QNN CPU Conv/ReLU sample with an eight-second
execution budget: 2329 inferences, successful exit. Samples include startup,
teardown and measurement overhead; this was a response check, not an energy or
performance benchmark. current_avg and power_avg lagged the phase transitions.

Samsung-specific units
Despite their names, /sys/class/power_supply/battery/power_now and power_avg
return system CURRENT in mA on this firmware. The installed sec-battery and
max77775-fuelgauge drivers confirm the mapping. See driver-evidence.txt and the
preserved modules/disassembly. Do not interpret these fields as uW or mW.
Periodic fuel-gauge logs also expose labeled ISYS/ISYSAVG (mA), VSYS (mV),
IIN (mA), VBYP (mV), and QH (uAh). Those log snapshots are not synchronized with
the load test. ISYS * matching VSYS could form a system-node power estimate,
but its physical coverage and accuracy still need external validation.

Perfetto and PowerStats
The 30-second trace contains 121 samples per battery track. Scheduler events
wrapped its ring buffer and removed the early phase markers. samples.tsv retains
all three phases independently; do not treat this trace as a complete sched trace.
An eight-second power-only capture confirmed 33 samples per battery track with
the same outcome: power_rail_empty_packet=1, entity_state_residency_invalid=1,
and no rail-energy or energy-estimation tracks. No AIDL/HIDL PowerStats HAL was
found registered. The framework powerstats service exists but its dump was empty.
This establishes no usable rail telemetry through these interfaces on this build,
not proof that the silicon lacks measurement hardware.

The apparent batt.power_mw Perfetto track is computed from abs(voltage*current)
by the trace processor. It is not an independent hardware power measurement.

Inventory
12 power-supply devices: battery, ac, usb, wireless, pogo, otg, max77775-charger,
max77775-fuelgauge, max77775-otg, mfc-charger, sc8583-charger, sec-direct-charger.
25 PMIC ADC channels: 16 temperature-labeled and 9 voltage-labeled channels.
VPH_PWR readings were approximately 3.97 million raw units, consistent with uV,
but not externally calibrated. Some temperature-labeled channels appear to
return thermistor ADC values: do not convert all of them directly to degrees C.
115 regulators expose voltage settings and load requests, not measured rail power.
77 thermal zones include CPU/GPU/NSP/DDR/PMIC sensors, plus unavailable sentinel
readings, BCL levels, and trip-related entries. Those are classified in summary.json.
qti_btl temperature extrema/counters are exposed but not validated as live readings.
Additional bounded searches found SDAM NVMEM paths, without a usable rail-energy
interface. Raw PMIC registers, calibration values and NVMEM were not accessed.

Next measurement step
Capture Monsoon output and USB input power together with the workload to validate
the phone telemetry and account for both power paths. Monsoon alone measures its
own supplied path while USB can contribute separately. The known failure to start
reliably without USB must be resolved before attempting a single-supply experiment.
No cables, charging controls or Monsoon settings were changed for this test.

Final state
Root retained; SELinux Enforcing; same boot; tracing off; test workload stopped.
No firmware, module, clock, thermal or charging configuration changes were made.

Evidence

samples.tsv: 96 timestamped samples, including original raw Samsung field names.
power-only.perfetto-trace: small confirmation capture suitable for Perfetto.
power-only-tracks.csv and power-only-errors.csv: parsed confirmation results.
